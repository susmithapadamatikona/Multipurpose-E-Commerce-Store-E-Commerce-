OUR BEGINNINGS

Built to Eliminate the Noise of Endless Warehouses

In early 2020, three design and logistics veterans found themselves deeply frustrated by modern digital marketplaces. What began as a decade of experience in retail soon evolved into a mission: protect customers from misleading listings, overwhelmed product feeds, and delivery promises that rarely deliver.

We identified shoppers deserved better. We founded Stackly to bring curated goods, reliable details, and transparent value together in one place. We work with makers and brands who care about quality, so every product has earned its place through lasting utility and timeless design.

CURATION STANDARDS

Our Product Philosophy

Every item on OmniCart passes through a rigorous four-phase intake funnel. Less than 4% of examined products make it into our catalog.

01

Product Discovery

Seeking timeless aesthetics, modular repairability, and durable utility across emerging boutique designers and established engineering houses.

02

Quality Checks

40–day rigorous real-world torture tests: stress-testing hinges, material washing trials, thermal stability, and drop resilience.

03

Brand Partnerships

Direct relationships with verified makers guarantee fair worker wages, transparent supply chains, and authentic serial verification.

04

Customer Feedback

Continuous catalog pruning. Any item that dips below a 4.5 customer rating is pulled and reassessed with the manufacturer immediately.
