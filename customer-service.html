HELP CENTER

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about orders, payments, and delivery.

AllOrdersShippingReturnsPaymentsAccountProduct
How do I track my OmniCart package in real time?⌄

Every shipped order includes an automated FedEx, UPS, or USPS tracking link accessible on your Recent Orders page. You’ll also receive milestone SMS updates when your parcel is out for delivery.

What is the return window and are returns free?⌄

Most items can be returned within 30 days. Eligible returns include a prepaid label or QR drop-off code at no cost.

Can I cancel or modify an order after clicking place order?⌄

You can cancel or update an order while it is still being prepared. Open Recent Orders and choose the eligible item to see available actions.

How does the OmniCart 30-Day Price Match Shield work?⌄

If an eligible item’s price drops within 30 days of purchase, contact support with your order number and the current listing for a review.

What should I do if my payment failed but my card was charged?⌄

Some banks show a temporary authorization while a payment is pending. It normally clears automatically; contact us if it remains after seven business days.

Are international duties and customs fees included at checkout?⌄

Where available, duties and taxes are calculated at checkout and displayed before you place your order.
